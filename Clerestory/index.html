Clerestory

A modular panel for your Mac.

A file shelf and handy shortcuts in a panel at the top of your screen. Drop files, folders, text or links on the Shelf while you work, then drag them where they need to go.

Meeting notes

Everything within reach

Clerestory lives in the menu bar and opens from the top center of any display.

Shelf

A holding spot for files, folders, text, links and images. It points to your files instead of copying them. Search with ⌘F, preview with Space and undo a removal with ⌘Z.

Open it your way

Point at or click the top center of the screen, drag a file there, or press ⌘⇧Space. Press it twice quickly to save your clipboard to the Shelf. Pin the panel to keep it open.

Notch companion

On Macs with a notch, a small counter beside the camera shows how many items are on your Shelf.

Battery

Charge, power draw over the last two minutes, and battery health. Switch Low Power Mode from the panel with the optional helper.

Quick Settings

Arrange the panel's module shortcuts and get to common settings without opening a window.

Experimental modules

Turn on Transfers, Timer, System Status, Now Playing, Clipboard History and AI Usage in Settings → Modules.

Careful with your files

A regular drop only remembers where a file is. Nothing gets moved, and removing it from the Shelf never deletes the original.

  • Hold ⌘ to move a file in. Clerestory keeps a checked copy and puts the original in the Trash, where you can restore it.
  • It keeps track of originals. If a file is renamed or moved, its Shelf item still finds it.

Private by design

Your Shelf stays on your Mac.

  • No account, analytics or telemetry. Clerestory goes online only to check for updates, and you can turn that off.
  • Sandboxed. It only touches files you drag in or pick, and needs no Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission.
  • Clipboard History is opt-in. It records nothing until you turn on Record Clipboard, and skips copies password managers mark as private.

Get started

Download the DMG from the latest release, open it and drag Clerestory to Applications. Open Clerestory from Applications; a short tour shows you around the first time. Updates arrive through Check for Updates… in the menu bar icon's menu.
